# Activity — Design Your Own Pipeline

**Your goal:** sketch a small production pipeline for something you make repeatedly. (Process over
product — a clear chain of steps beats a polished single output.)

---

## Part 1 — Name the source of truth

Pick a multi-step deliverable you produce more than once (a newsletter, a lesson, a report
packet, a proposal).

**Write its source of truth** in one line — the standard or brief it must match.

```
Deliverable: _________________________________________________________________

Source of truth: _____________________________________________________________
```

---

## Part 2 — Break it into stages

List **3–5 stages** that turn the input into the finished thing (e.g., outline → draft → review →
format).

```
Stage 1: _______________   Stage 2: _______________   Stage 3: _______________

Stage 4: _______________   Stage 5: _______________
```

---

## Part 3 — Mark the checkpoints

Go back through your stages. Mark which ones **AI does** and put a **✔ where YOU must review.**

**Write down:** the stage you'd never let AI do without your review.

```
✔ Must-review stage: _________________________________________________________
```

---

## Part 4 — Run one stage

Pick one stage and run it with AI. Then **check its output against your source of truth.**

**Write down:** did it match the standard? What drifted?

```
______________________________________________________________________________
```

---

## Reflection (the one thing to take with you)

Finish this sentence:

> **"The stage I'd never let AI do without my review is ____________, because ____________."**

---

## Discussion questions

1. What do you make repeatedly that could become a pipeline? What are its stages?
2. Which stages would you happily automate, and which demand your eyes?
3. Errors compound down a chain — where is an early mistake most dangerous?
4. Where have you seen speed *without* checkpoints go wrong?
