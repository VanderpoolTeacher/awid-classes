# Activity — Turn a Repeated Task Into a System

**Your goal:** turn one repeated task of yours into the *start* of a system. (Process over
product — a rough template beats a perfect one-off here.)

---

## Part 1 — See the drift

Ask a chatbot to make something for you (e.g., "write a short weekly update"). Then ask for the
**same thing again** in a fresh chat.

**Write down:** how were the two results different?

```
______________________________________________________________________________
```

> 💡 That drift is exactly why a system beats repeated one-off requests.

---

## Part 2 — Build a template

1. Name a task you do **more than once** (a weekly update, a lesson, a proposal, a post).
2. Find or make **one good example** of it, and ask the chatbot: *"Strip this down to a reusable
   template — replace the specific content with clearly labeled placeholders."*
3. Use that template to generate **one** new instance.

**Write down:** your repeated task, and whether it came out more consistent than a from-scratch
request would have.

```
My repeated task: ____________________________________________________________

More consistent than from scratch? ___________________________________________
```

---

## Part 3 — Push an improvement back

Look at your new instance. Find **one thing** you'd want in *every* future version.

**Write down:** what you'd add to the **template** (not just this one output).

## Reflection (the one thing to take with you)

Finish this sentence:

> **"A task worth turning into a system is one where ____________________."**

---

## Discussion questions

1. Where do you keep re-making similar things from scratch? What would a system save you?
2. A system costs more up front and less every time after. How do you decide when that's worth
   it?
3. Why push improvements *back into the template* instead of just fixing each output?
4. What's one messy pile you could have AI help you organize *before* building a system on it?
