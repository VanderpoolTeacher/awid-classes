Organize Your Work

🗂️ A story to start with

Open your Downloads folder. Be honest. It's probably a graveyard — hundreds of files named document(3)-FINAL-v2.pdf, screenshots from months ago, things you'll never find again.

The frustrating part: the tools to stay organized have always existed. Folders aren't new. The hard part was never the technology — it was us.

That changes when you pair a little structure with AI, which is happy to do the tedious part.

The mess is a habit problem, not a tools problem

We've always had folders. Digital life turns into a junk drawer because organizing is boring work we skip. The fix isn't more willpower — it's a simple structure plus a helper (AI) that doesn't mind the boring part.

Work in project folders — with a structure

Give each effort its own project folder, everything in one place. AI works far better pointed at one clear folder than at your whole scattered drive.

A pile in one folder is still a pile. Information architecture is the structure — a logical place for each thing, consistent names. Let AI help: "here's my mess, propose folders and a naming convention."

Keep a log

A short log of what you did and why (and what you asked AI) sounds like overhead — it's the opposite. It's how future-you understands the work without re-doing the thinking, and how you answer "why did we do it this way?" months later.

Two lines a day. (This course was built with exactly that kind of running log.)

Keep a sandbox — and why this is the foundation

A sandbox folder is a no-stakes place to experiment and break things on purpose. Don't run experiments inside your real work — play in the sandbox, then promote what works.

Organization isn't busywork. Templates live in folders; repeatable systems run on organized inputs. Get this base right and the powerful stuff stops being fragile.

You don't fix it with one heroic cleanup that lasts a week. You fix it with a little structure and a helper: point AI at the mess, let it propose folders and names, keep real work in project folders, jot a two-line log, and play in a sandbox. The pile stops growing because the system — not your willpower — keeps it in order.

Remember:

  • The mess is a habit problem — AI can do the tedious part.
  • Project folders + an information architecture.
  • Keep a log and a sandbox.
  • Organization is the foundation templates and systems stand on.
