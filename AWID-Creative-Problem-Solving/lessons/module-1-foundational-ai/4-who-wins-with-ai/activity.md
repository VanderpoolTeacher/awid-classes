# Activity — Steer the Tool With What You Know

**Your goal:** feel how *your* clarity and expertise steer AI. (Process over product — notice the
difference your input makes.)

---

## Part 1 — Vague vs. specific

1. Paste any paragraph into a chatbot and ask it to **"make this better."** Read the result.
2. Ask again, but be **specific** — give a length, an audience, and what to keep
   (e.g., *"cut to 100 words for a sixth-grade reader; keep the three main points"*).

**Write down:** which result was more useful, and what made the difference?

```
______________________________________________________________________________
```

> 💡 You didn't need to be technical — you needed to be *clear.*

## Part 2 — Use your expertise

Pick something you genuinely know well (a hobby, your job, a subject).

1. Ask AI to help you with it.
2. **Catch one thing** it got wrong, too generic, or missed.

---

## Discussion questions

1. Where might *your* expertise give you an edge with AI that a "tech person" wouldn't have?
2. When is "just give me the default" fine, and when do you need to steer?
3. Agents can take real actions for you. What would you let one do — and where would you insist
   on approving every step?
4. How do you know when you've hit the edge of your expertise and should bring in a person?
