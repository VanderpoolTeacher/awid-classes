# Activity — Build the "Before You Paste" Reflex

**Your goal:** practice deciding what's safe to put into AI. The win is the *check* before you
paste — not a polished output.

---

## Part 1 — Anonymize it

Find or invent a short piece of text with personal info (a name, an email, a phone number).

**Rewrite it** replacing every personal detail with a placeholder — "Person A," "email@—,"
"555-XXXX." That anonymized version is what's safe to paste.

```
Original (do NOT paste this into AI):
______________________________________________________________________________

Anonymized (safe to paste):
______________________________________________________________________________
```

> 💡 This is the placeholder trick: AI helps, but never sees the private parts.

---

## Part 2 — Read the fine print

Open the **terms or privacy page** of an AI tool you use. Find **one line** about what they do
with what you type.

**Write it down:**

```
______________________________________________________________________________
```

Were you surprised? ☐ Yes ☐ No

---

## Part 3 — Safe vs. never

Make a quick two-column list. Put **3 things** in each.

```
SAFE TO PASTE                         NEVER PASTE
1. _________________________          1. _________________________
2. _________________________          2. _________________________
3. _________________________          3. _________________________
```

## Reflection (the one thing to take with you)

Finish this sentence:

> **"Before I paste anything into AI, I'll ask myself: ____________________."**

---

## Discussion questions

1. Where's *your* line between "convenient" and "I shouldn't paste that"?
2. When private data leaks into an AI tool, whose responsibility is it — the user, the company,
   or both?
3. Free tools are free because something pays for them. What are you trading, and is it worth it?
4. If you worked somewhere with strict data rules, how would you still get AI's benefits safely?
