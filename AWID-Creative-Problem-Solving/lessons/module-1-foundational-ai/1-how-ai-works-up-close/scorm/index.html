How AI Works, Up Close

🧠 A story to start with

You're deep in a long chat. Early on you said, "I'm writing for sixth-graders, keep it simple." Forty messages later, it's handing you dense, jargon-packed paragraphs — like it completely forgot.

It didn't get dumber. It ran out of memory.

A quick look under the hood — memory, context, images, and where its knowledge runs out — turns confusing moments into predictable ones.

AI has a limited working memory

An AI has a context window — a limited amount of text it can pay attention to at once. Your whole current conversation lives inside it.

When the chat overflows the window, the earliest things — like your opening instruction — quietly drop out. It can't "see" them anymore. Like cramming so much into your head that the first details slip away.

Work with the memory

Once you know that, the fixes are obvious:

  • Restate key context when it matters.
  • Start a fresh chat for a new task instead of bloating one.
  • Keep important instructions near where you need them.

You're not fighting the tool — you're feeding its memory on purpose.

Context is how you aim it

AI can't read your mind. The more it knows about your goal, audience, and situation, the better it helps.

"Write something about dogs" leaves it guessing. "A friendly 100-word intro about dog adoption for a shelter newsletter" gives it everything it needs. Context is the aim.

Images, and the edge of its knowledge

Image generation isn't drawing line by line — it computes a whole field of pixels into something you recognize, astonishingly fast. Impressive, not magic.

And its knowledge is a huge but finite pile, frozen at training time (models are even running low on fresh text). Big, but bounded — and it may miss recent events.

🧠 Back to the forgetful chat

Now you know what happened: the conversation overflowed its context window, and your opening instruction fell out. And you know the fix — restate it, or start fresh. A frustrating mystery becomes a two-second move.

Remember:

  • The context window is AI's limited memory — long chats forget the earliest things.
  • Work with it: restate key context, start fresh for new tasks.
  • Context is how you aim it — goal + audience + constraints.
  • AI's knowledge is big but finite and frozen; images are fast computation.
