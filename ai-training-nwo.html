The Landscape at a Glance

A growing, multi-layered ecosystem

  • Universities lead on formal credentials. BGSU, University of Toledo, and Tiffin all now offer AI-branded degrees, minors, and graduate specializations.
  • Tiffin University has the deepest AI portfolio — a full B.S. in AI & Prompt Engineering, an AI+X minor, and multiple certificates.
  • Community colleges fill the workforce gap. Owens runs affordable non-credit AI series for the public.
  • A vibrant non-academic scene anchors the region — Great Lakes AI Week, EmpoweredAI, AI OWL, Anthony Wayne Innovation & Design, and The AI Collective Toledo.
  • Notable gaps: no NW Ohio AI Meetup group, and the public library teaches no AI classes.

What We Did NOT Find

Notable gaps

  • No NW Ohio AI Meetup group. The only Ohio AI/ML Meetups are in Cleveland, Columbus & NE Ohio. Local grassroots activity runs through The AI Collective (via Luma), EmpoweredAI, and AWID — not Meetup.
  • Toledo Lucas County Public Library teaches no AI classes in its standing catalog (MS Office, Google Workspace, 3D printing, etc.). It offers LinkedIn Learning & O'Reilly online, and has hosted an EmpoweredAI kids program — but no library-taught AI class.
  • Eventbrite is thin. The only verified NW Ohio listing is the templated LearneRRing workshop. A "FutureReady AI Prompt Engineering Bootcamp" surfaced but was too low-reliability to confirm.
  • Unconfirmed institutions: No AI-specific offerings verified for Lourdes University, Defiance College, Northwest State CC, Terra State CC, or Penta Career Center — worth a direct check.

Method & Caveats

How this was built

Two deep-research passes fanned out across universities, community & technical colleges, career centers, event platforms (Eventbrite, Meetup), libraries, chambers, coworking spaces, and commercial trainers. 209 sources were searched; 50 claims went through 3-vote adversarial verification — only those backed by primary sources are shown here.

  • Time-sensitive: Great Lakes AI Week 2025 & Owens' Feb–Mar 2026 sessions have passed; the 2026 conference agenda is not fully verified.
  • Self-described programs (EmpoweredAI, AI Collective cadence) should be reconfirmed against current calendars.
  • Not exhaustive — reflects only claims that survived verification.

Execution · Governance, Roadmap & Outcomes

How we stand it up

Phase 10–6 months
Formalize & pilot. Sign an MOU and stand up a 3-partner steering council. Launch a co-branded pilot workshop series and a shared regional AI-training calendar.
Phase 26–18 months
Credential the pipeline. Owens packages AWID + EmpoweredAI content into a stackable micro-credential; enroll employers in Ohio TechCred; pilot a K-12 → community → college pathway.
Phase 318+ months
Scale & sustain. Employer cohort programs, dual-enrollment for high schoolers, and a diversified funding base (TechCred + grants + sponsorship) that makes the model self-sustaining.
Measure success by: Learners reached Credentials earned Employers engaged Equity / underserved reach Small businesses adopting AI
